Our Story

Before candles were décor,
they were presence.

Long before fragrance was sold in a jar, it was lit at a doorway to welcome a guest, carried through a home before a festival, offered quietly at the end of a day. In India, light and scent have never been separate things — a lamp was never just light, and an attar was never just a smell. Both were ways of marking a moment worth remembering.

ABSOLUT CANDLES was built on that instinct. We work the way a fragrance house does — sourcing sandalwood, vetiver, rose and resins the way they have always been sourced — and shape that knowledge into a candle for a modern room.

From Flame to Fragrance

An idea that kept evolving.

01

The First Light

Fire began as necessity — warmth, safety, a way to see through the dark. It was humanity's first shared ritual.

02

Ritual & India

Light became ceremony. Lamps at doorways, oils on skin, incense at prayer — flame turned into welcome, and welcome turned into fragrance.

03

The Modern Candle

Flame moved indoors, into wax and wick — no longer just light, but atmosphere. A room could now hold a mood on purpose.

04

ABSOLUT

We took that heritage of fragrance and gave it a modern form — candles built the way a perfume is built, note by note.
